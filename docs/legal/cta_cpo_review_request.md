# CTA / CPO Scoping Review Request

**Purpose:** Request engaged counsel review whether the program described
below implicates Commodity Trading Advisor (CTA) or Commodity Pool Operator
(CPO) registration topics, and what facts or thresholds would change the
analysis.

**Status:** Scope note only for current internal / personal trading
use. Not a pre-code, pre-paper, or pre-live blocker under current
scope. This request is reactivated only on a scope-expansion trigger
(client / outside / pooled capital, paid signals, paid advice,
copy-trading, public performance marketing, managed accounts).

**Trigger:** Legal review becomes mandatory if project scope expands
beyond internal / personal owner capital.

This document does not assert a legal position. All determinations are
reserved to engaged counsel.

**Issued:** 2026-05-09
**Owner:** Legal — Appendix I scoping (per `docs/OWNERS.md`)
**Program reference:** Build Plan v1.4-r1; errata §4

---

## 1. Program Description

The program is a systematic trading effort developed under Build Plan
v1.4-r1. The initial instrument scope is MES (CME E-mini S&P micro
futures). Trading is fully systematic with rules registered in a strategy
registry prior to evaluation, and with a documented validation pipeline
that includes baselines, out-of-sample partitions, and family-level
holdback. The intended capital base at the present scoping moment is
internal-proprietary only — that is, capital owned by the trading entity
and its principals. No external or client capital is in scope at this
phase. No solicitation, marketing, or track-record sharing is contemplated
at this phase. The program operates through a single trading entity which
will be identified in the questionnaire response (see
`legal_scope_questionnaire.md`, Section B). Personnel are limited to the
principals and a small set of contractors and advisors identified in the
questionnaire (Section D).

## 2. Scope Question

We ask counsel to consider, on the facts as described above and as
elaborated in the accompanying questionnaire:

- (a) Whether the program as currently scoped implicates CTA registration
  topics, and if not, what change of facts or what threshold (number of
  accounts advised, type of capital, holding-out, compensation structure,
  or other factor) would put the question back on the table.
- (b) Whether the program as currently scoped implicates CPO registration
  topics, and if not, what change of facts or what threshold (pooled
  vehicle formation, commingling, third-party participation, or other
  factor) would put the question back on the table.
- (c) Whether any related self-regulatory or exchange-rulebook topics
  (NFA membership, exchange-level registration or notice obligations)
  warrant attention at the current phase.
- (d) Whether the answer changes if the program later expands beyond MES
  to additional instruments described in the questionnaire (Section C).

We are not asking counsel to render a binding opinion at this stage; we
are asking counsel to identify the topics, the relevant tests, and the
fact patterns that would change the answer.

## 3. Exemption Analysis Request

We ask counsel to identify and review the exemptions that may be relevant
to the facts above, including but not limited to those typically considered
in proprietary-capital scenarios (for example, the family of exemptions
under CFTC Regulation 4 — including 4.13(a)(3) and any cognate provisions
counsel believes relevant). For each exemption counsel believes worth
considering, we ask counsel to identify:

- the conditions for availability,
- any required filing or notice,
- the renewal or affirmation cadence,
- the events that would cause loss of the exemption,
- the recordkeeping implications of relying on the exemption.

We are not asking counsel to confirm reliance on any specific exemption in
this document; we are asking counsel to map the option space.

## 4. Record-Keeping Implications

For each registration regime or exemption regime counsel identifies, we
ask counsel to identify the corresponding records the program should
preserve, the retention period, and the format / accessibility
expectations. This output will be cross-walked against the program's
existing record artifacts (strategy registry, contamination log, fills,
position log, order audit trail, research reports, sign-off artifacts) to
confirm coverage.
