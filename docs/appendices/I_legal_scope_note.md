# Appendix I — Legal Scope Note

**Status:** Scope control, not legal advice. This note replaces the prior
counsel-sign-off gate role of Appendix I for the current internal-only
scope.

---

## Current scope

This project is an in-house / personal trading bot using only owner
capital.

- Internal / personal capital only
- No client accounts
- No outside capital
- No pooled capital
- No paid signals
- No paid futures trading advice
- No public marketing of performance
- No managed accounts
- No copy-trading service

Legal review is **not** required for internal testing, paper, or live
trading under this scope.

## Scope-expansion triggers (hard stop)

If any of the following enters scope at any point, work pauses
immediately and does not resume until counsel review is complete:

- Client capital
- Outside capital (including friends-and-family or any third party)
- Pooled capital (commingled accounts, fund-of-one, master-feeder, SMA
  aggregation)
- Paid signals
- Paid futures trading advice
- Copy-trading service
- Public performance marketing (including blogs, social posts, podcasts,
  newsletters, public talks, or any external communication that
  references returns, methodology, or principals in their trading
  capacity)
- Managed accounts

The scope-expansion path uses the existing scope-expansion templates
under `docs/legal/` (`legal_scope_questionnaire.md`,
`cta_cpo_review_request.md`, `counsel_signoff_template.md`). Those
templates remain available for that case; they are not part of the
internal-only gate set.

## What this note is not

- Not legal advice.
- Not a substitute for counsel review when scope expands.
- Not a sign-off authorising any of the listed scope-expansion items.

## Sign-off block

This note has no signer. It is informational scope control. The Director
Sponsor acknowledges the current scope is internal-only and is
responsible for raising the hard stop if any trigger above enters scope.
